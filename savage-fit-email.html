Fueling the Transition

As we move from winter into spring, your body needs nutrient-dense foods that support muscle building while keeping you energized. Here's what I've been making on repeat:

Pre-Workout Power Bowl

Ingredients:

• 1 cup cooked quinoa
• 4oz grilled chicken or tempeh
• ½ sweet potato, roasted
• Handful of spinach
• 2 tbsp tahini
• Squeeze of lemon
• Sea salt, cracked pepper

Why it works: Perfect macro balance for glute-focused training. Complex carbs for energy, lean protein for recovery, healthy fats for hormone support. Make it Sunday, eat it all week.
